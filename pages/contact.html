What a visit looks like

  1. 1. Welcome beverage and an aromatic sample tour of whole herbs and pods.
  2. 2. Walk into the stone chakki room where small batches turn at low temperature.
  3. 3. Comparative aroma tasting: single ground versus signature blended profiles.
  4. 4. Sit with the trade desk and discuss volumes, condiments, and custom labels.
  5. 5. Leave with your bespoke signature blend freshly sealed.
